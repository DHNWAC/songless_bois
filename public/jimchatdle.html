How to play

  1. A real message from the group chat is hidden. Tap or type letters to reveal it.
  2. Each wrong letter costs a life. Run out and the message is revealed anyway. Shorter messages get more lives.
  3. Hit I know who said it whenever you're ready. You get 3 calls. A wrong call sends you back to the letters.
  4. A right call doesn't end the game. You still finish the message.
  5. You get two scores out of 50 that add up to 100. Phrase rewards solving with lives to spare. Sender rewards calling it early and in fewer calls.
  6. There's one new message each day. Copy your result and brag in the chat.
