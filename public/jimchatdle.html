How to play

  1. Three rounds a day: Jimseng (the group chat), Movies and Speeches. One quote each.
  2. The quote starts with just its first word or two. Guess who said it.
  3. A wrong guess or a skip shows more of the quote. You get 6 turns, and the last one shows the whole thing (plus the film, in Movies).
  4. The earlier you get it, the more you score: 100, 80, 60, 40, 25, then 10.
  5. Finish all three for your daily total out of 300, then copy it to the chat.
