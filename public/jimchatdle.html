How to play

  1. Pick a mode: a message from the group chat, a famous movie line, or a famous quote. It's hidden. Tap or type letters to reveal it.
  2. Each wrong letter costs a life. Run out and the message is revealed anyway. Shorter messages get more lives.
  3. Know it already? Hit Solve it and type the whole thing. The more that's still hidden, the bigger the bonus. A wrong answer costs 2 lives.
  4. Hit I know who said it whenever you're ready. You get 2 calls. A wrong call sends you back to the letters.
  5. A right call doesn't end the game. You still finish the message.
  6. You get two scores out of 50 that add up to 100. Phrase rewards solving with lives to spare. Who rewards calling it early and in fewer calls.
  7. There's one new puzzle per mode each day. Copy your result and brag in the chat.
